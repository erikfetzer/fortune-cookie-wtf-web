Level 2: the cookies they baked for you

Sites can't read each other's cookies, but the big ad platforms will hand you the profile they built from theirs. Drop it in and I'll read it back to you. It's processed on your device and never uploaded.

  1. Meta: in Accounts Center, open Your information and permissions, then Download your information. Pick "Ads information" in JSON format, then drop the files below.
  2. Google: open My Ad Center, copy the topics and brands it lists, and paste them below.
  3. Anything else: paste any list of "interests" a site says you have, one per line.

Level 3: the cookie jar

A website can only read its own cookies. A browser extension, with your permission, can read all of them. So we built one, so you can see what everyone else already sees.

Cookie Jar is a free browser extension that opens the jar for real. It shows every company that has left a cookie in your browser and who's watching on the page you're on right now. No guessing from hints this time. It reads the actual cookies.
